tag: '1-week sprint'
cardProblem: "Vendor onboarding takes 15 to 30 business days because no one owns the vendor's status across six functions."
problem: >-
  Indian enterprises take 15 to 30 business days to onboard a vendor. Six functions touch each one, and no one owns its status end to end. A wrong tax detail can cost the buyer its input tax credit.
insight: >-
  We interviewed procurement, business, legal and finance teams in automotive, shipping and construction. Onboarding failed the same two ways everywhere: no shared status, and documents checked by eye. Both come from one gap: nobody owns the steps in between.
decision: >-
  Start with larger Indian automotive enterprises running SAP. Run cheap live checks on every shortlisted vendor, and the costly ones only on the vendor who wins. The trade-off: India and automotive only, so slower early growth in return for a sharper first market.
mvp: >-
  Invites by email and WhatsApp; live PAN, GST, Udyam and company-registry checks; one status tracker showing who holds the record; bank and filing checks after award; a trusted record pushed to SAP. Not built: sourcing, invoicing, contract management, ongoing monitoring.
measure: >-
  Days from shortlist to a trusted SAP record, split into days waiting on the buyer and on the vendor.
