cardProblem: 'A citizen who seeks help from the police often leaves with no record that they were heard, or what happens next.'
cardLine: 'Prototype in progress'
problem: >-
  People who seek help from the police often leave with no proof they were heard, no readable record, and no clear next step. It falls hardest on those without confidence, legal knowledge or time to keep returning.
insight: >-
  The citizen is asking three questions: did the system register that I came? Is this what was recorded? What happens now, and what do I do? No primary interviews fitted the sprint, so this rests on published research and one detailed real journey, and the PRD says so.
decision: >-
  Build a layer around the official record, not a police system. Station staff publish only fixed, citizen-safe status updates, never free text. The trade-off: staff can't write a personal note in v1, but nothing internal can reach the citizen by mistake.
mvp: >-
  One station and one journey: lost property after a difficult cab ride. A visit reference created before the visit; a plain-language view of what was recorded, which the citizen can confirm or flag; current status with one next action; a light console for station staff.
measure: >-
  Share of interactions where the citizen can verify what was recorded and what happens next. A proposed measure, not a result.
